Florida, circa 1600 — At the Dawn of Spanish Colonization

#30DayMapChallenge 2026 · Day 10: Prompting Only

A satellite-style reconstruction of an inhabited landscape: pine savannas, forests, wetlands, Indigenous towns and a small Spanish colonial presence.

Built in Python from historical ecology and archaeological sources. This is a regional interpretation, not historical satellite imagery: vegetation dates are proxies, some sites are approximate, and coastlines and fine textures remain uncertain. An accompanying provenance map shows where the evidence changes.

Created through prompts to OpenAI Codex, which researched, wrote and ran the workflow and checked the resulting map. Day 10 theme: https://30daymapchallenge.com/#2026-themes
